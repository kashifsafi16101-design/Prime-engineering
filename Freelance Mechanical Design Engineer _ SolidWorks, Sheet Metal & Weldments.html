FAQ

Common questions

Do you approve safety-critical designs?

I provide design, modeling and drawings. For safety-critical or regulated products, final approval must come from a licensed engineer or the relevant standards body, and I design my work to support that review.

How is pricing set?

You get a fixed price after I review your brief. It is based on scope and complexity, and it will not change unless you change the scope.

What files will I receive?

Native SolidWorks files, STEP files for any CAD software, and PDF drawings. STL files are included for 3D printing projects.

How long does a project take?

It depends on size and complexity. Small parts can take a few days. I give you a firm timeline with every quote.

Can you work from my sketch or a physical part?

Yes. Hand sketches, photos with measurements, and existing parts are all fine starting points.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. Revisions are included in every quote, and the number is agreed before we start.

Is my design kept confidential?

Yes. I am happy to sign an NDA before you share anything.
